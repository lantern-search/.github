# Lantern

Lantern is an independent research lab working on the market for transaction ordering.

The order of transactions inside a block is worth money, and on every major chain a market has grown up to sell it. Searchers compete for position, builders assemble blocks, validators choose among them, and users pay for the whole arrangement, usually without seeing the price. We study how that market clears: who captures the value, by which route, and at whose expense.

The work spans Ethereum's builder auctions and Solana's leader-scheduled blocks, where the same contest is settled in a few hundred milliseconds. We build our own measurement from the chain up.
